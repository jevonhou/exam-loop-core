# Code of Conduct

We want technical discussion in this project to be safe, specific, and useful.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss behavior and evidence without personal attacks;
- welcome questions from people with different levels of experience;
- respect privacy, content ownership, and disclosure boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and publication of another person's private information;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and licensing even when a proposal is declined.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer or security channel. Do not expose personal details in a public issue. Enforcement decisions should minimize disclosure and protect affected people.
