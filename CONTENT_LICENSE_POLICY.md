# Content License Policy

Exam Loop Core source code and content packages are licensed independently.

Every distributed content package must include:

- a stable package identifier and version;
- the content copyright holder or source;
- an SPDX license identifier or a complete custom license notice;
- attribution and modification notices required by its source license;
- confirmation that redistributing prompts, answers, explanations, and media is permitted.

Keep the evidence behind those declarations: source URLs or contracts, acquisition date, original copyright notice, license text, attribution requirements, modifications, and the person who reviewed redistribution rights. The repository provides a [content provenance review template](docs/content-provenance-template.md) for this purpose.

The engine's MIT license does not grant rights to any content loaded into it. Do not contribute copied exam questions, commercial study-book material, restricted datasets, personal data, or generated material whose redistribution rights have not been checked.

Schema validation checks that license metadata is present and syntactically plausible. It does not verify ownership, license compatibility, exam accuracy, official status, or legal permission.

Repository examples must be small, fictional, and original. They are demonstrations of the package format, not claims of exam accuracy or official endorsement.
